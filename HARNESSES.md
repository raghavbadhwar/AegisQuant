# AegisQuant Harness Contract

The sole orchestration runtime is LangGraph. Nodes receive and return typed state updates. Tool access is capability-scoped by mode. Replay and historical modes are network-denied. Specialist nodes cannot size portfolios or call brokers. The coordinator cannot synthesize the final forecast. The CIO cannot add facts. Evidence Auditor and Forecast Verifier may block or force abstention. Bull and Bear opening reviews are independent. Candidate generation cannot promote changes.
