# Roadmap

The authoritative capability plan is `docs/BUILD_SPEC.md` and its acceptance matrix is `docs/TRACEABILITY.md`.

Shipped in the MVP: deterministic replay/backtest cycle, simulated broker, LangGraph specialist desk, raw-first source intelligence, evidence claim graph, governed PIT memory with optional GBrain boundary, locked research lab with qtype/purged-CV validation, human-only promotion decisions, and a read-only dashboard.

Future work may improve licensed data, model providers, source workers, evaluation depth, and operations. It must preserve paper-only execution, sealed historical replay, locked risk/holdout/promotion components, and explicit human promotion.
